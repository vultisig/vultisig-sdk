'@vultisig/sdk': patch
---

CCTP bridge/claim builders (`buildCctpBridge`, `buildCctpClaim`) now resolve chain names through the SDK's canonical `normalizeChain()` instead of exact-matching raw caller strings against the registry. `getCctpChain()` accepts tickers, aliases, and natural-language phrasings (`"eth"`, `"Arbitrum One"`) the same way every other first-party chain-name consumer does, the returned envelopes report the canonical chain name, and same-chain alias pairs (`"Ethereum"` vs `"eth"`) are now correctly rejected as identical instead of silently building a same-chain bridge.
