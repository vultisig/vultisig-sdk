Replace `VaultSend`'s and `VaultSwap`'s local, already-drifted `tickerMap`/`decimalsMap` copies with a shared `getChainTicker`/`getChainDecimals` helper backed by the SDK's canonical `chainFeeCoin` registry. The local maps had already fallen behind (missing `Ton`, `Noble`, `Tron`, `CronosChain`, `Dydx`, `Kujira`, `Mantle`, `Hyperliquid`, and `Sei`, with `VaultSwap`'s decimals map missing even more and silently falling back to `18`), which fed amount parsing and coin construction with the wrong ticker/decimals for those chains.
