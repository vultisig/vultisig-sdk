`decodeFromToolResult` only recognized legacy snake_case tx metadata (`from_chain`, `unsigned_payload`, `cosmos_payload`), even though newer SDK-owned surfaces (`normalizeTx`, the signable-transaction contract) already emit and consume the camelCase equivalents (`fromChain`, `unsignedPayload`). Callers on those surfaces had to re-shape their canonical envelopes back to snake_case before the decoder would accept them. The decoder now accepts both the snake_case and camelCase aliases for the chain hint and payload fields.
