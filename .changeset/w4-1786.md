Teach `decodeCosmosTx` (the shared bytes-oracle behind `decodeFromToolResult`) to decode `MsgBeginRedelegate` and `MsgWithdrawDelegatorReward` into the canonical `Envelope`, with two new `EnvelopeKind` values (`redelegate`, `withdrawReward`). The SDK could already build these two staking actions but not decode them back, so downstream safety surfaces (CLI WYSIWYS, app decoded-intent card, co-sign gate) degraded them to `unknown`. Redelegate's recipient is the destination validator and its source validator is preserved in `validatorSrcAddress`, so changing the source cannot produce an equivalent canonical envelope. Withdraw-reward's amount is left empty since the reward is computed on-chain, not carried on the wire. Cosmos governance vote decoding is intentionally left out of this patch — the SDK's vote envelope carries `proposalId`/`voteOption` fields that don't fit the existing `Envelope` shape (recipient/amount/asset), so adding it needs a shape decision rather than a mechanical decode addition.
