Add `extractCctpMessageFromReceipt`, a pure helper that decodes the canonical `MessageSent(bytes)` log from a CCTP burn tx receipt and returns the raw `message` bytes plus its `keccak256` `messageHash`. Previously every first-party consumer had to hand-roll this log decode/hash step to bridge `buildCctpBridge`'s burn output into the attestation poller and `buildCctpClaim`.
