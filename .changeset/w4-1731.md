`parseActionDisplay` (StakeKit action display canonicalization) now degrades per-step instead of all-or-nothing: a step that fails to canonicalize (e.g. an unsupported network like Cardano, or a malformed EVM step) falls back to its decoded form, while every other step in the same action stays canonicalized. Previously, one unsupported step nuked the canonicalized shape for the entire action, pushing deterministic parsing of the other (supported) steps back onto app/backend consumers.
