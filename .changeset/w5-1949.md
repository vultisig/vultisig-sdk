Adds the AgentRouter / USDC checkout canonical primitive family under `@vultisig/sdk`'s EVM tools (`evm.*` / top-level): a USDC payment-chain registry (`USDC_PAYMENT_CHAIN_CONFIG`, `USDC_CONTRACTS`, `CHECKOUT_CHAIN_IDS`, `lookupUsdcPaymentChain`, `resolveUsdcPaymentContract`, `resolveUsdcPaymentChainId`), `formatCheckoutUsdcDisplay` (the half-away-from-zero microdollar → USD display formatter), and `encodeAgentRouterDepositWithMemo`/`decodeAgentRouterDepositWithMemo` (ABI-driven via viem, so decode now also recovers the memo, not just the token/amount head). Previously these lived as two independently-drifting hand-rolled copies in `vultiagent-app` and `agent-backend-ts` — a display-formatting mismatch between the two already produced a real bug (app issue #2528).
